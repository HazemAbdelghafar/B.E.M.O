Amazon:    
    Amazon Product Advertising API:
        - Need to have an affiliate account which is free to create but you need to have a website, To request for access to PA API you must:
            - Have completed 3 qualifying sales in 180 days.
            - Have an approved associate account.
            - Comply with the associates program Operating Agreement.

    Third Party APIs:
        - All are paid (too much) and their free tier is not enough for the project.
        - They have a lot of restrictions.
        - Free third-party APIs are not reliable, or require an Amazon PA API key.

    Web Scraping:
        - Looks like the only option.
        - Lots of headache (terms of service, rate limiting, CAPTCHA).
        - But can be done with only static scraping.
        - So limited functionality.
        - Needs to be updated frequently.

    Actions:
        - Search for items on Amazon. (List) 
            - Search by keyword.
            - number of items to return.
            - Filter by:
                -   Free Shipping?
                -   Fulfilled by Amazon?
                -   Min Price
                -   Max Price

        - Lookups for a specific item. (Get) (list with n=1)
        
        Data:
        - Product Name
        - Product Price
        - Product URL
        - Product Images
        - Product Description
        - Product Rating
        - Product Reviews
        - Product Brand
        - Product Category
        - Product ASIN
        - Product Details
            - Dimensions
            - Weight
            - Color
